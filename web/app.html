How the numbers are madesources and simplifications
Annuity pricesModel fitted to 30 Hargreaves Lansdown best-buy rates (22 Oct 2026, within 2.4%), then shifted with the live 20-year gilt yield and implied inflation from the Bank of England. Your own quotes override it.
MarketsUK equities, gilts, cash, CPI and long gilt yields each year since 1872 (Jordà–Schularick–Taylor Macrohistory Database, extended to 2025 with published figures). Simulations stitch together 5-year blocks of real history; "like today" futures open with a real decade that began in similar conditions.
People and taxONS 2022-based life tables. rUK income tax, thresholds frozen to April 2031. State Pension £241.30 a week. Scottish tax and inheritance tax on pensions from April 2027 are not modelled.
